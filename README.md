# Sales-Superstore-Data-Analysis

A data-driven analytical project conducted as part of the Purwadhika Job Connector Data Science and Machine Learning program. This portfolio leverages the **Sales Superstore dataset** to uncover insights that can drive strategic business decisions related to sales performance, customer segmentation, discount strategies, and operational efficiency.

---

## Project Overview

In today’s competitive retail environment, understanding customer behavior, optimizing product offerings, and making data-informed decisions are crucial. This analysis explores sales trends, product performance, and customer segments across various regions and product categories using statistical methods and machine learning techniques.

---

## Goals

- Identify top-performing and underperforming product categories and sub-categories.
- Analyze customer behavior based on segments, regions, and purchasing frequency.
- Evaluate the impact of discount strategies on profit margins.
- Assess the contribution of shipping modes to operational efficiency.
- Apply LRFM (Length, Recency, Frequency, Monetary) analysis for customer segmentation.

---

## Problem Statements

- Do high discount rates negatively impact profitability?
- Which products and regions drive the most sales and profit?
- Which sub-categories are consistently unprofitable?
- How does shipping method affect order performance and costs?
- How can customers be segmented to enhance targeting and retention strategies?

---

## Key Analyses

- Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
- Profitability and Sales Trend Analysis
- Discount vs Profit Correlation Study
- Regional Performance Mapping
- LRFM Clustering with K-Means
- Dimensionality Reduction with PCA
- ANOVA and Correlation Tests

---

## Insights & Results

- Certain sub-categories such as *Phones* and *Chairs* drive substantial profit, while *Tables* often incur losses.
- Excessive discounting is correlated with decreased profit margins.
- The *West* region and *Consumer* segment contribute the highest revenue.
- LRFM segmentation reveals actionable customer clusters (e.g., Loyal Customers, At-Risk, Big Spenders).

---

## Further Strategy

- Reevaluate discounting schemes to prevent margin erosion.
- Prioritize high-margin products and reconsider poor performers.
- Launch targeted marketing campaigns based on LRFM segments.
- Explore logistics optimization based on shipping mode insights.
- Integrate sentiment analysis on customer feedback for service improvement.
